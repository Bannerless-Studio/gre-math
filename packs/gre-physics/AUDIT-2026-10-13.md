Independent Opus 5.5 audit of the published 1,452 items. Read-only: no item file was edited.

## Verdict

- **Blind re-solve: 143 of 143 sampled keys agree.** No KEY WRONG and no ILL-POSED item.

## Recommendations, in priority order

1. **Undisclosed variable names (55 items):** fix the whole class, not item by item. Either add "Type $ as `kB`" to each prompt, or show the declared `vars` under the input on expression cards (one change in `engine/app.js` `updateEcho`). The second fixes all 55 plus any future item. Better still, also accept common aliases (`T_1`→`T1`, `mu_0`→`mu0`, `k_B`→`kB`).
2. **Exact-form measured values (10 items, all T2):** switch to `form: "decimal", rel: 0.02` per CURRICULUM §5. `t2-0033` first.
3. **Micro purity:** convert some of the 18% statement-recall tier b items (list above) to one-step numeric or expression micros. Or move them to tier a. That also adds typed items, which the production gate needs.
4. **Small fixes:** set `t2-0015` to d2, trim `t1.graphs` to 80 words or fewer, and re-align `why` in `t7-0011`, `t7-0029`, `t7-0037`, `t7-0038` and `t6-0021`.

**No full re-solve.** 0 of 143 blind keys were wrong.
